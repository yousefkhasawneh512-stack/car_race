# Road and terrain report (v41, 2026-10-17)

What the game's roads, junctions, speed bumps, terrain and buildings are built from, what was checked against data, and what is an
estimate. "Verified" below means "taken from a mapped OpenStreetMap tag"; nothing was surveyed on the ground.

## 1. Road network

Source: OSM highways (`osm/gen_world.py` + `osm/net.py` → `03a_net_data.js`), 26k junctions, 4,608 km of road in a 29.2 × 24.4 km window.
`tests/val_net.js`: 400 of 400 random routes within 6 km of Al-Balad are routable (avg detour 1.30); 99.4 % of edges are one connected network.

| Attribute | In OSM (of 14,546 ways in the window) | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry, class, names | all / all / 588 named | **verified**: centrelines and junctions as mapped |
| One-way | 2,109 | **verified** (`oneway`) |
| Bridges / tunnels / layer | 79 / 33 / tagged | flags kept (77 bridge, 33 tunnel edges); **drawn at ground level**: no deck heights or ramps are modelled (see §6) |
| Lanes | 8 | used where tagged (`lanes × 3.3–3.4 m`) |
| Width | 0 | **estimated**: class defaults, e.g. residential 6.5/8 m, secondary 8.5/11 m (one-way/two-way, `net.py` W1/W2) |
| Speed limit (`maxspeed`) | 12 | used where tagged; **estimated** elsewhere: class defaults (trunk 80, primary/secondary 60, tertiary 50, residential 40 km/h). OSM's 3 speed cameras (maxspeed 80) are recorded in `OSM_CALM` |
| Surface | 195 | **not used**. Grip comes from `surfK()`: road 1.0, track 0.75, off-road 0.65/0.9 (heuristic) |
| Lighting (`lit`) | 0 | street lights are generated along roads (decorative) |
| Roundabouts | `junction=roundabout` | 142 clustered roundabouts from the data. Three were **named by hand** (University, Al-Qubbah, Al-Thaqafah Circle) and are unnamed in OSM (`manifest/unresolved_places.json`) |
| Traffic signals | 14 signal nodes in OSM | 177 signalised junctions: **10 OSM-mapped**, the rest **inferred** (three or more arterial arms meet; `05b_decor.js`) |
| Road markings | not mapped | generated by road class (centre/edge lines, zebras at junctions): decorative, not survey data |

## 2. Speed bumps (v41)

- OSM in the whole play area has **2** traffic-calming features (`traffic_calming=hump` node 6587046542 and `traffic_calming=yes` node
  7241900188; Overpass 2026-10-17, `fetch.py traffic_calming`). Both are now placed as **real** bumps (`estimated:false`, snapped to the
  nearest road within 25 m).
- The other **88 are estimates**, as before: local or service roads near OSM-mapped schools, hospitals, clinics and parks, at least 115 m apart
  and never within 115 m of a real one. They are labelled estimated in code (`b.estimated`), in the console log and in the on-screen attribution.
- Behaviour: each bump arms once (it must be left by 2.7 m) and has a 1.2 s cooldown, so one crossing registers once. Arcade: hop
  0.16–1.0 m/s. **Realistic handling:** the suspension absorbs it (heave + pitch impulse, per-car spring frequency); hop 0 below 29 km/h,
  ≤ 0.7 m/s at speed (`tests/handling_test.js`: 30 km/h → 0.00 m/s and 2.5 cm body heave; 70 km/h → 0.32 m/s, 5.3 cm). Damage (Cosmetic/Full)
  only above about 58 km/h (16 m/s), once per crossing.
- Geometry: a 0.72 m deep, 0.10 m high striped hump at the road centre, as wide as the carriageway (88–95 %). It is lifted by the terrain at
  its centre; on a 10 % grade the hump's ends differ from the road by about 3.6 cm (not tilted to the slope).

## 3. Terrain

- NASA SRTM v3, 1 arc-second, sampled on a **1 km grid** with bicubic interpolation (`03d_terrain_data.js`). Narrow wadis and cuts are
  smoothed out; places span about 710 m of elevation (−243 to +466 m relative to Al-Balad; the highest terrain lifted is +512 m).
- Everything is authored flat and lifted once (`applyTerrain`). `tests/terr_check.js`: **0** of 894 landmark/prop groups have items off the
  terrain; the ground mesh matches `TH` exactly (max |Δ| 0.000 m).
- Buildings on slopes: each box gets a **skirt** down to its lowest corner (48,374 skirted instances), so no building floats. The uphill side
  of a big footprint sits partly into the ground; that is the approximation, not a bug.

## 4. Buildings

- 46,000 real OSM buildings (19,483 BBBike extract + 26,432 outside it since v40). `tests/bld_check.js`: 95.9 % of footprint pieces are
  placed; every placed piece sits exactly at its OSM centre with its data height (31 overlapping duplicate outlines, one of each kept); no
  building centre on a carriageway. Dropped: 1,934 that would cross a road, 35 inside landmark sites, 26 overlapping; 6,861 shrunk ≤ 40 % to
  clear a road edge (OSM outlines and estimated road widths overlap).
- Footprints are **rotated rectangles** (1–8 per building), not exact outlines. Heights come from OSM `height` (213 buildings) or
  `building:levels` (860); **all others are estimates** by type, size and density (seeded per building, as in `conv.py`). OSM has no roof tags here.
- Facades are a procedural shader by style (old-town stone, shopfront, apartment, villa, institutional …). They are **not** photographic
  reconstructions. Hand-modelled landmarks: Dar As-Saraya (black basalt with arches and corner towers, matching the Commons photos in
  `manifest/photos.json`), Beit Arar, Beit Nabulsi, the clock tower, Wasfi Al-Tal Square and Emaar Park; since v40 the heritage houses stand on
  their real footprints. No new landmark models were added in v41.

## 5. Rendering and performance

- Chunked instancing with view culling (v40): 538–1,060 draw calls and 83k–281k triangles at the four `dc2` spots (v39: 2,110–2,577 / 1.3 M).
- Lighting, materials and shadows were **not changed** in v41 (a sun + hemisphere rig with optional HDR bloom; `02b_sky.js`, `12b_post.js`).

## 6. Not done (needs data or a separate, larger change)

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Bridge / tunnel decks at real heights with ramps | OSM gives `bridge`/`layer` but no deck heights; needs a profile model and approach ramps, then re-running `terr_check` |
| Denser terrain (250 m or better) | needs a licensed DEM processed offline; the 1 km grid misses wadis |
| OSM `surface` grip | only 195 of 14,546 ways are tagged; would need a `03a` format change for little coverage |
| Real road widths / lane counts | OSM has 0 widths and 8 lane tags here |
| Photo-based facades | photos are reference only (a network host would break the single-file rule) |
| Real-device frame rate (Android, iPhone) | no devices available in this environment |
